# Roadmap

One step at a time — each is built, played, and signed off before the next starts.

1. **Model battles** — ship-to-ship combat with wind, cannons, and AI.
   Includes boarding and capturing enemy ships (instead of only sinking
   them) and a captain's duel to decide who keeps the ship.
   *Status: core combat in place; boarding/capture and duels next.*

2. **World map** — a Caribbean map with historically-placed Spanish,
   British, French, and Dutch ports and colonies.

3. **NPCs & factions** — a top-10 wanted pirates list, pirate havens, an
   admiral per nation, regional commanders, and a mayor for each town.

4. **Diplomacy** — deeper than the original Pirates!: reputation per
   nation, consequences for who you raid, and relations that shift over
   time rather than staying fixed.

5. **Towns & economy** — ship upgrades, a goods market with real supply
   and demand, hiring crew, and companions who grant passive bonuses
   (e.g. better sailing).

6. **Land battles** — skirmishes and siege-style assaults on towns.

7. **Captain's career** — treasure hunting (maps, buried caches), an
   aging captain, retirement, and a scored ending.

8. **Foundations** *(cross-cutting, not sequential)* — save/continue so
   progress persists between sessions. Worth deciding early since it
   shapes how game data is structured, rather than bolting it on later.

## Build notes

- Switched the renderer from 3D to 2D top-down (from "Broadside"'s
  original 3D prototype) for full 360° situational awareness around the
  ship — a fixed chase camera can't show what's to your sides on any
  device, which matters a lot for an arcade-style game.
